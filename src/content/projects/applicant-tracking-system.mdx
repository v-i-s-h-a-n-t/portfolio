## Overview

USP ATS is an AI-assisted applicant tracking system for University of the South Pacific job postings. Students build profiles and apply; administrators manage jobs and review applications with AI scoring assistance.

## Objectives and requirements

- Let students sign in, build profiles, search and filter job postings, submit applications, and track or withdraw them.
- Let administrators create and manage postings, require a second administrator's approval, review applications ranked by AI score, and update application status.
- Show scoring details including eligibility, qualification and motivation fit, and missing requirements.
